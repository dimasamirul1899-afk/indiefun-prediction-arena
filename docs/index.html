Bet on the SOL price. Settle on-chain. Audit everything.

A parimutuel prediction market on Solana devnet. Every bet is a real SOL transfer to a market vault with an on-chain memo — winners split the whole pool, no house fee, and every transaction links to Solscan. Built for the Indie.fun Hackathon (Superteam Earn).

1 · ConnectPhantom on devnet, or use demo mode — no wallet needed.
2 · BetPick YES or NO. Your SOL goes to the market vault with a memo.
3 · ResolveAfter the deadline the market settles against the SOL/USD price.
4 · Get paidWinners split the entire pool pro-rata. Every payout is on-chain.

How the vault works (read this). Each market has its own vault address. Bets are plain SOL transfers to the vault plus a memo (ARENA:v1|bet|<market>|YES|NO) — anyone can audit a market on Solscan. The vault key lives in your browser (localStorage) because this is a devnet demo with no backend; after the deadline, the vault holder clicks Settle and every winner is paid from the vault. The vault key can be exported and published after settlement so anyone can verify the vault emptied exactly. Devnet only — SOL here is free testnet money with no value.
